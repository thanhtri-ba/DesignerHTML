How we run

Four rules, written on the back of a flapjack wrapper in 2016 and never changed since.

  1. Uphill

    We run the hills on purpose. Flat ground is for recovery days and car parks.

  2. Nobody last

    A sweeper runs at the back of every group, so nobody ever finishes alone.

  3. Any weather

    Rain, wind, fog or snow. The hill never cancels, so neither do we.

  4. Loud summits

    Cheer, shout and sing badly at every summit. The hill can take it.

Your first Saturday

No forms, no fees, no fitness test. Here is everything that happens, in order.

  1. Turn up

    North gate of Kestrel Common at 06:50. Look for the yellow flag.

  2. Say hi

    Tell anyone in a yellow top it's your first time. That is the whole sign-up.

  3. Pick a group

    Four pace groups from walk-run to quick. Swapping groups halfway is allowed.

  4. Climb and cheer

    Three hills, one summit shout, then coffee at the gate café.
