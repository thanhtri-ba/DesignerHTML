Questions, answered

Anything else? Write to us or ask at the desk — someone is there from seven in the morning.

Do I need any dance experience?

No. Foundations and the Improvisation Lab are built for complete beginners, and most of our regulars started there.

What should I wear?

Anything you can move in. We dance barefoot or in socks. The changing rooms have showers and lockers.

Can I cancel a booking?

Up to four hours before class, and the credit goes straight back onto your pass.

Is the building accessible?

Step-free entrance, a lift to both studios and an accessible changing room. Tell us what you need and we will prepare the room.

Do you teach private lessons?

Yes, with most of our teachers, in Studio Two. First dances for weddings included — no judgement.

How do I hire a studio?

Send us your dates and a line about what you are making. We reply within a working day.
