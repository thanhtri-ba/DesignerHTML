From brief to print

Every course follows the same twelve weeks, whatever you are making. Crits every Thursday, proofs every Saturday.

See the fees
  1. Weeks 1–2

    Brief

    A real client or a real cause. You write the brief back in one sentence before you draw anything.

  2. Weeks 3–5

    Sketch

    Fifty thumbnails, pencil only. We pin them to the wall and choose three together.

  3. Weeks 6–9

    Grid & type

    The idea meets a grid, a typeface and four inks. Weekly crits keep it honest.

  4. Weeks 10–12

    Print

    Proofs, corrections and a final run in the studio downstairs. Then the show, and a glass of something.
